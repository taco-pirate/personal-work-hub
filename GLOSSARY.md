# Personal Work Hub

The private work of one person: what they owe, and what they will do next. A colleague has their own hub.

## Language

**Reply**:
The answer owed when a message asks the user for a decision or a reply. It is ready only when every promised fact was decided by the user, and it stays owed until the user marks it sent.
_Avoid_: Ready reply, draft reply, email response

**Task**:
A deed the user owes that is not an answer owed to someone. It stays owed until the user marks it done.
_Avoid_: Action item, to-do, follow-up, commitment

**Source**:
The message or meeting an item came from. Items that share a Source are linked, and finishing one does not finish the others.
_Avoid_: Thread, original email
