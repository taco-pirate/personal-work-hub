# Personal Work Hub

The private work of one person: what they owe, and what they will do next. A colleague has their own hub.

## Language

**Reply**:
The answer owed when a message asks the user for a decision or a reply. A Reply is ready only when every fact it promises has been decided by the user; anything undecided stays visible as a placeholder. The user sends it.
_Avoid_: Ready reply, draft reply, email response
