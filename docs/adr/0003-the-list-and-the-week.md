# The home screen is the owed list plus the rest of this workweek

The screen the user clears is the Desk. A row above the list counts owed items, Escalated items, Replies, Tasks, and usable time left this workweek. Those counts are not performance measures. The list shows owed Replies and Tasks. Escalated items come first. Dated items follow, earliest Due first. Items with no Due follow those, oldest first. Beside the list, usable gaps run from now through the last configured workday of this week, today first. A Due after this week stays on the list, and its gap waits until that week is the current one. A day named with no time is Due at the configured end of that calendar date, including a date that is not a workday. Two dates that disagree leave Due empty. The panel shows usable gaps of at least 15 minutes and does not place work into them or create a calendar event. A date outside the workweek does not add a day to the panel.
