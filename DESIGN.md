# Design

How the Personal Work Hub screens are arranged. What is owed, and how time is counted, stay in `PRODUCT.md`. The words stay in `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Shell

A sidebar and one content gutter. The sidebar is Home, the owed-work page, and Calendar. It folds to an icon rail. This version has no inbox, projects, members, teams, or settings.

The shell, the count rows, the list and board switch, and the board are Arc components, including Arc Pro where that block is Pro. The density is a calm workspace: warm off-white, Geist, small type, one shared gutter, quiet borders, light and dark.

## Home

Home is the Desk. Above the owed list, a row counts what is owed, what is Escalated, how many are Replies, how many are Tasks, and how much usable time remains this workweek. Those counts are not a score. The usable gaps sit beside the list.

## Owed work

The owed-work page shows the same owed Replies and Tasks as the Desk, without the gaps. Sent, done, and dropped items stay off both pages. The page can show that set as a list or as a board.

## Calendar

Calendar is the week, full width. Above it, a row counts meetings still ahead, the hours those meetings take, usable time left, Focus Time left, and how many meetings are Uncategorized. Those counts are facts about the week.
